How this works

Henry is rebuilding camp as a 3D model. Pick a place from the menu. Each view shows the real photo on the left and the model on the right; switch views with the numbered buttons. Drag the 360° views and both turn together. Add a pin where something's wrong, write notes, attach your own photos if they help. Everyone who reviews sees each other's notes. No login needed.
