# circus

## LOCKED decisions (Henry-confirmed: never revert without asking him)
- Built 2008, attached to the Sports Center (shared west wall) but works as its own building. Circus program was in the Rec Hall through 2007.
- Model permanent structure only: no silks, nets, mats or rigging that come and go.
- Walls inside: navy with white stars. Posts alternate red / yellow. Blue diagonal braces only in the bays the 360 shows.
- Above the lower wall: plain blue band, then the window band (two rows of panes), then a star band up to the rafters. NO stars in the gable / rafters (plain navy).
- Roof structure: triangular trusses with a flat bottom chord under a gable roof, closely spaced. NO lamella vault.
- Windows are real openings: you must see outside / the Sports Center / sky through them, never the star wall behind glass. Both end walls have TWO rows of windows.
- East wall: does not reach the floor; open along the bottom about 2.8 m (NOT 5.2 m) with a raised section in the middle. No triangle in the gable.
- Doors on the long walls are star-painted and blend into the wall (outlines + seam only, no brown/red door slabs).
- Post counts measured in the 360 (v8, awaiting review): north wall ~13 bays (~2.6 m), south wall 10 bays (~3.4 m). North: west bay open, and from ~31 m east to the NE corner the lower bays are open (5 bays). South: west bay open with a low opening.
- Shared west wall: lower ~4.6 m is open framing (posts, knee walls/benches, one walk-through), blue band, then star panels.
